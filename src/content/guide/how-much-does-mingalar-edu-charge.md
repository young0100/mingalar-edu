Mingalar Edu service fees depend on the product you choose. Contact our team for the current quote and a written breakdown of the support included in your programme.

## What to confirm before payment

Ask for a written service scope and fee breakdown for your chosen product. Confirm the payment stages, when each stage becomes due and the conditions that apply if an application is unsuccessful.

## Included support and separate costs

The listed service support covers shortlisting, documents, applications, admissions follow-up, visa documents and pre-departure planning. Tuition, school application fees, translation or notary costs, flights, insurance and housing are separate costs.

School tuition, entry requirements and exchange rates still need confirmation. Contact our team for the information relevant to your chosen school and intake.

## Payment records

Only official accounts and receipts are valid. Confirm the payment instructions directly with Mingalar Edu and retain your written service agreement and receipts.
